Playback and analysis

Playback removes each channel’s constant DC offset and uses one gain per file to target RMS 0.1, with a 0.89 peak ceiling. Uncheck “Match playback level” to hear the original gain and DC offset. Signals below 8 kHz are resampled to 48 kHz for browser playback, preserving pitch and duration. Raw WAV downloads preserve the original samples and sample rates.

Switch A/B continues at the same elapsed time, clamped to the shorter signal’s duration. Independent stochastic outputs and generated cohorts have no samplewise correspondence. Plots normalize level; texture descriptors describe signal differences and do not establish perceptual equivalence.
